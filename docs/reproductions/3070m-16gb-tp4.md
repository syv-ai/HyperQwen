# Reproduction: 4x RTX 3070M 16 GB (TP=4), no P2P, PCIe 3.0 x8, every setup

*October 2026. Docker Compose, the published image at `ce9615c` (vLLM 0.31.0). Four Dicasver RTX 3070M 16 GB
boards (GA104M, the laptop chip on a desktop PCIe card, sm86), TP=4, no P2P between any of them.*

[← back to the reproductions index](README.md)

Setups A to E from the [main README](../../README.md#which-setup-do-i-want), each as shipped, plus what A needed
to run. It is a data point for the README's "Four Ampere cards" question (#135: two 3090s gave less aggregate
throughput than one), on different cards (115 W, 16 GB) and with no single-card run of these cards to compare against.
The same box at TP=2 was measured the same day, and the scaling table below sets the two against each other.

## Method

The README's field-test protocol per setup: boot, `bash bench/run_benchmarks.sh single` (or `batch --prefill
--long`) twice, keep the second run, then `bench/quality_battery.py <tag> --gsm-only`. Nothing else ran on the
cards. `nvidia-smi dmon` logged power, clocks and PCIe throughput through every run.

## Setup A (batch)

**As shipped it does not boot at TP=4.** CUDA-graph capture passes, but the JIT kernel warmup that follows runs
out of memory: `CUDA out of memory. Tried to allocate 62.00 MiB ... of which 57.06 MiB is free`, after the
launcher's `GPU_UTIL=0.94` had given the KV cache 10.1 GiB per card (a 1,278,865-token pool).

With **`GPU_UTIL=0.90`**, nothing else changed: boots in 183 s with a 1,200,000-token pool (9.48 GiB per card),
serves both passes and GSM8K without a restart. The rows below are that configuration, so they are not the
untouched setup A (the same change as on TP=2).

## TP=2 to TP=4 on the same cards

Second runs, greedy unless noted; the TP=2 runs used cards 0 and 1 of this box (`3070m-16gb-tp2.md`, filed in its
own PR, so the file is here once that one is merged).

| | TP=2 | TP=4 | TP=4 / TP=2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| B, C1 decode | 103.3 | 127.2 | 1.23x |
| B, C8 decode | 306.9 | 343.6 | 1.12x |
| D, C1 decode | 98.8 | 138.9 | 1.41x |
| D, C8 decode | 411.3 | 477.0 | 1.16x |
| E, C1 decode | 93.9 | 128.0 | 1.36x |
| A (`GPU_UTIL=0.90`), 64 concurrent, e2e | 683.9 | 771.0 | 1.13x |
| A (`GPU_UTIL=0.90`), prefill 1k / 100k | 1,147 / 810 | 1,312 / 1,078 | 1.14x / 1.33x |
| A (`GPU_UTIL=0.90`), 1x100k TPOT | 25.6 ms | 16.2 ms | 0.63x |
| KV pool, D / E | 382,914 / 565,472 | 1,164,572 / 1,993,718 | 3.0x / 3.5x |

Two more cards buy 12-41% of decode speed and three times the pool. Doubling the cards does not halve the step
time: each step still makes 128 all-reduces (two per layer), now across four ranks through host memory instead of
two. Prefill gains more the longer the prompt (+14% at 1k, +33% at 100k).

## Quality

The 200-question GSM8K runs read 0.940-0.960. **D at TP=4 read 0.940, below the 0.950-0.965 that the field-report
template gives for every shipped configuration on this harness**, so it was run again over the full 1,319-question
test set: **0.951** (1,254 correct), against **0.955** (1,260) for the same D at TP=2 on the same box. Six answers
apart over 1,319 is noise, so the 0.940 is sampling noise in the 200-question subset; nothing points at a TP=4
numerics problem (MTP acceptance is 2.60-2.65 tokens per step at TP=4 and 2.61-2.64 at TP=2).

## Power, link and what limits this box

- **Below the power limit at TP=4**: loaded samples averaged 77-81 W (max 104 W on B-E, 114 W on A) against the
  115 W limit, at a 1.92 GHz SM clock, 66-70 °C average, 84 °C peak.
- **The all-reduce is NCCL's SHM transport over PCIe 3.0 x8** (no P2P): `dmon` showed peaks of 2.3-3.7 GB/s per direction on the
  single-user setups and 4.7 GB/s on batch, against 1.2-1.9 and 4.4 GB/s at TP=2.
