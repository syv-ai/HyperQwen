*October 2026. Docker Compose, `--profile single`, vLLM 0.31.0. Two RTX 5070 Ti (sm120, Blackwell).*

## Boot

- KV cache size: **762,831 tokens** (max-model-len 200,000, 3.81x concurrency)
- No OOM; healthcheck reports `healthy`, `/health` returns HTTP 200
- GPU memory: ~15.5 / 16 GiB used per card (~250-295 MiB free)
- Note: 0.96 is above the repo's measured-safe MTP floor of 0.93 (gotcha 4). Soak before trusting for sustained long generations.

## `bench/run_benchmarks.sh single`, greedy (T=0) and default sampling

Decode rate = C × 1000 / median TPOT. Eight real-prompt cohorts (8 prompts, 1024 output tokens).

| cohort | T=default e2e (tok/s) | T=default decode (tok/s) | T=0 (greedy) e2e (tok/s) | T=0 (greedy) decode (tok/s) | mean TTFT (ms, default) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| C1 | 44.71 | 74.7 | 79.45 | 78.4 | 8,940 |
| C2 | 73.93 | 75.1 | 78.86 | 78.4 | 12,205 |
| C4 | 75.52 | 76.7 | 79.75 | 81.8 | 30,536 |
| C8 | 75.76 | 76.7 | 79.08 | 79.7 | 49,264 |

Note: The high TTFT at C4/C8 is due to the huge-context profile's prefill queue (200k max-model-len), not a stall. Decode throughput is roughly flat across concurrency (75-80 tok/s), consistent with the single-user MTP profile being decode-bound at batch 1.

## Notes

- This is a 2x RTX 5070 Ti (sm120 Blackwell) setup, a card not yet in the reproductions table.
- The 0.46 GiB VRAM reservation on card 0 (from the cosmic-greeter display daemon) was not freed during this run; freeing it (stop cosmic-greeter + nvidia-drm.modeset=0) would allow slightly higher GPU_UTIL.
- KVarN pool: 762,831 tokens, well above the 200,000 max-model-len.
- No quality check (GSM8K) was run.
